- God's Eye View is Cape Dispatch: `/api/cape/*` talks to Fire Wire via
  `FIREWIRE_URL` (no default to the retired capemaydispatch.com site). Scanner
  pins and CAD clips open the RADIO panel for Fire Wire chunk audio; empty
  feeds stay quiet until station SDRs arrive. Cape CCTV catalogs from Fire Wire
  only; fx stills absolutize onto that host for `/api/cctv/frame`. Cape
  May–Lewes Ferry webcams are always seeded into the Cape CCTV pack and the
  Webcams layer, so ferry HLS streams open in the right-hand camera panel even
  when Fire Wire is offline.
