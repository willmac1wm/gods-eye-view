Color keys LEGEND

Most county layers share one color scale, so a color means the same thing everywhere. Each item also carries a status badge that says where the information came from.

County severity scale

  • Blue: normal, reporting, nothing wrong
  • Yellow: advisory, action stage, a few customers out
  • Orange: watch, minor flooding, 50+ out
  • Red: warning, moderate flooding, 500+ out, crash or closure
  • Purple / magenta: major flooding, extreme warning
  • Grey: not reporting in the last 3 hours

Airport weather (METAR)

  • Green: VFR
  • Blue: MVFR
  • Red: IFR
  • Magenta: LIFR
  • Grey: no recent report

Status badges on every card

  • Live feed fetched recently
  • Official issued by a government agency
  • Observed a measurement
  • Forecast an agency forecast, not yet happened
  • Estimate modelled by the site, never a measurement
  • Reported from a utility, TomTom or a scanner
  • Transcribed machine transcript of radio audio
  • Unverified community information
  • Reference static locations and boundaries
  • Delayed last good copy kept because the feed is late

Aviation 12 LAYERS

Live Flights

flights

Aircraft positions with heading. Tap one for callsign, altitude, speed, squawk and route. Emergency squawks are flagged.

Source
OpenSky Network (ADS-B and Mode S from volunteer receivers), adsb.lol fills gaps. Every 30 s.
Limits
Only what volunteer receivers hear. Low GA traffic along the coast drops out. Routes come from a cache and are hidden when implausible.

Military Flights

military

Aircraft adsb.lol tags as military: Coast Guard helicopters out of Air Station Atlantic City, 177th Fighter Wing F-16s at ACY, Navy traffic offshore.

Source
adsb.lol, every 15 s.
Limits
Many military aircraft don't broadcast ADS-B.

Local ADS-B

local-adsb

Aircraft heard by the site's own 1090 MHz and 978 MHz UAT receivers, which catch low traffic the internet feeds miss. Empty until a receiver is connected.

FAA Airport Status

faa-status

FAA NAS Status: pins ACY, PHL and EWR when they have a ground stop, ground delay program, closure or delay, plus nationwide counts.

Source
nasstatus.faa.gov, every 3 min.

Local TFRs

cape-tfrs

Temporary flight restrictions near the county drawn as boxes: VIP movement (91.141), disaster or hazard areas (91.137), space operations (91.143), air shows (91.145) and security TFRs (99.7).

Source
FAA TFR list.
Confirm
The NOTAM is the authority: tfr.faa.gov.

Military / USCG Areas

military-areas

Military and Coast Guard ground property, such as Training Center Cape May. Orange outline for USCG, grey for other military.

Source
OpenStreetMap, volunteer-mapped. Every 6 h.
Note
Land boundaries only. It is not airspace.

Prohibited & Restricted Airspace

drone-airspace

FAA Special Use Airspace polygons of type P (prohibited) and R (restricted).

Source
FAA SUA data, hourly.
Limits
Only P and R areas. Warning areas off the NJ coast, MOAs and alert areas are not drawn. Check NOTAMs for whether an area is active.

SIGMETs

sigmet

International SIGMET areas drawn as raised polygons. Tap for the hazard and valid time.

Source
Aviation Weather Center international SIGMET product.
Limits
Covers oceanic FIRs such as New York Oceanic. Domestic SIGMETs, Convective SIGMETs and G-AIRMETs over the U.S. are not in this layer. Use aviationweather.gov for those.

Airport Weather

airport-weather

METARs for 18 nearby airports, colored by flight category. Tap for wind, visibility, temperature, altimeter and the raw METAR.

Map key
Green VFR, blue MVFR, red IFR, magenta LIFR, grey stale.
Source
aviationweather.gov, every 10 min.
Ticker
KWWD's category, temperature and wind are in the top strip.

Airport Radio (ATC)

airport-radio

Atlantic City tower and approach audio from LiveATC, transcribed by the site. The map shows the newest transmission at ACY; the Calls tab lists the rest with audio.

Map key
Blue normal. Red when an emergency keyword matched (automatic, unconfirmed).
Limits
Machine transcripts mishear callsigns and numbers. Listen to the clip.

VDL2 Messages

vdl2

ACARS-style datalink messages from the last hour, heard by a VDL2 feeder near PHL. A list first; few messages carry a position.

Source
airframes.io, every 5 min.

GPSJam (Daily)

gpsjam-daily

Hexagons over the county colored by the share of aircraft reporting poor GPS accuracy over the previous day. 13 cells, nothing suspect for Nov 17.

Map key
gpsjam.org's scale: green low, yellow medium, red high. Green cells are drawn so "all clear" differs from "no data".
Source
gpsjam.org daily file, hourly check.
Limits
Yesterday's picture, not live.

Weather 10 LAYERS

Rain radar

weather-radar

NOAA MRMS radar mosaic over the globe. Standard reflectivity colors: greens light rain, yellow and orange heavy, red and magenta very heavy or hail.

Source
NOAA nowCOAST.

Satellite clouds

weather-satellite

GOES satellite cloud imagery. Good for nor'easter structure and fog banks over the bay.

Source
NOAA nowCOAST.

Lightning density

weather-lightning

GOES lightning mapper flash density. Brighter means more flashes.

Source
NOAA nowCOAST.

NWS Alerts

nws-alerts

Active alerts as shaded zones with the official text, instructions, severity and expiry. Includes Delaware Bay and the coastal waters.

Map key
Red warning, magenta extreme warning, orange watch, yellow advisory, blue statement.
Source
api.weather.gov, every 2 min. Never hidden by filters.

Wind

wind

Animated wind streamlines from a forecast model. This is a forecast, not observed wind.

Source
GFS / ECMWF IFS, hourly.

Wind stations

cape-wind

Observed wind at 4 NWS stations in and around the county.

Source
NWS observations.

Cyclone advisories

weather-cyclones

National Hurricane Center storms: position, forecast track and cone, with the advisory text.

Source
NHC / CPHC, every 5 min.

Air quality

air-quality

Monitor readings. U.S. stations use the AQI colors: green good, yellow moderate, orange unhealthy for sensitive groups, red unhealthy, purple very unhealthy, maroon hazardous.

Source
EPA AirNow and Environment Canada, every 20 min.

Space weather

space-weather

A label over the county: current Kp, activity level, the 3-day maximum and whether aurora could be visible this far south.

Source
NOAA SWPC.

Drought

drought

The county's U.S. Drought Monitor category (D0–D4), or "no drought", with the weekly release date.

Source
U.S. Drought Monitor, released Thursdays.

Ocean & Coast 11 LAYERS

Live AIS Vessels

ais-live-vessels

Ship positions with course. Tap for name, MMSI, type, speed and destination.

Source
AISStream live feed.
Limits
Small boats without AIS are invisible.

Marine Buoys

marine-buoys

6 stations: buoy 44009 (26 NM SE of Cape May), Cape May (CMAN4), Brandywine Shoal, Lewes, Atlantic City and 44091. Wave height and period, water and air temperature, wind, pressure.

Map key
Blue reporting, grey nothing in 3 h.
Source
NOAA NDBC, every 15 min. 44009 feeds the ticker's SEA item.

Tide & Flood Gauges

tide-gauges

32 tidal and river gauges colored by NWS flood category, plus the Cape May, Atlantic City and Lewes tide stations. Tap for observed level, forecast peak and that gauge's own official flood stages.

Map key
Blue no flooding, yellow action stage, orange minor, red moderate, purple major, grey not reporting.
Source
NWS NWPS and NOAA CO-OPS, every 6 min.
Note
Predicted tides are astronomical only and leave out storm surge. Compare a gauge only with its own stages.

Predicted Flooding (estimate)

predicted-flooding

When a tidal gauge is forecast to reach flood stage, orange shading shows the land that would be wet at that level above normal high tide (MHHW).

Source
NOAA sea-level-rise inundation maps plus NWS forecasts. An estimate, labeled as one.

Radar Flood (Sentinel-1)

sar-flood

Satellite radar "new water" over the county. Radar sees through cloud, so it shows actual standing water after a storm.

Source
Copernicus Sentinel-1. A pass every few days.

Water Gauges

water-gauges

5 USGS stream and water-level gauges with the latest reading.

Source
USGS Water Data, every 15 min.

Marine Warnings

marine-warnings

Coast Guard Broadcast Notices to Mariners for Sector Delaware Bay and District 5: aids to navigation out, hazards, closures. 200 in force. Only those with a position get a pin; the panel lists all.

Source
USCG NAVCEN, every 15 min.

Right Whale Speed Zones

whale-zones

The two NOAA Seasonal Management Areas off the county, with whether each is in effect today.

Rule
In season, vessels 65 ft or longer must go 10 kt or less (50 CFR 224.105).
Source
NOAA Fisheries.

Wildlife Sightings

wildlife-sightings

Recent wildlife observations and whale sightings with filters.

Source
iNaturalist and Acartia.

Ports

ports

Ports in blue and shipping chokepoints in amber, with channel depth where known.

Source
NGA World Port Index, a fixed snapshot.

Sea Marks

sea-marks

Buoys, lights and channel markers drawn like a nautical chart.

Source
OpenSeaMap tiles. Not for navigation.

Roads & Driving 9 LAYERS

Street Traffic

traffic

Road speeds drawn on the street network, colored from free-flowing to stopped.

Source
TomTom flow on OpenStreetMap roads.

Traffic Incidents

traffic-incidents

Crashes, closures, road works, flooding and lane closures with road, direction, delay and expected end.

Map key
Red crash or road closed, orange lane closed, blue flooding, yellow road works and everything else. Plain jams are left off; Street Traffic shows those.

Traffic cameras

traffic-cameras

Road camera stills. Tap a camera to see the latest frame.

CCTV

cctv

Public camera feeds, falling back to Street View where a camera has no image.

Webcams

cape-webcams

7 public webcams around the county. Good for a quick look at beaches, surf and weather.

Source
Windy webcams.

Transit

transit

Live bus and train positions from public GTFS-realtime feeds.

Bikeshare

bikeshare

Bikeshare docks with bikes available. Cape May County has no system, so expect nothing local.

Mapped ALPR Cameras

alpr-cameras

License-plate reader cameras mapped by volunteers, with the direction each faces.

Source
DeFlock / OpenStreetMap.

Fuel Prices

fuel-prices

A label over Court House with gas and diesel prices for the week.

Limits
EIA has no county or New Jersey gas price, only the Mid-Atlantic average. Tap GAS in the ticker for links to local station prices.

Public Safety 8 LAYERS

Police, Fire & EMS Calls

scanner-calls

Scanner calls transcribed into incidents with location, units heard, a summary and the audio clip.

Map key
Red high priority, amber medium, blue low, grey routine.

CAD incidents

cape-cad

Incidents built from dispatch traffic by the Fire Wire pipeline.

Dispatch radio

cape-radio

The county dispatch channels as playable audio.

Community Reports

community-reports

Reports residents send from the Report tab. They show only after a moderator approves them in /manager, and stay marked unverified.

Emergency Facilities

emergency-facilities

Hospitals (pink), EMS stations (orange), fire stations (red), police (blue) and hospital helipads (purple), with address and phone.

Note
These are not open shelters. Shelters come from County OEM.

Power Outages

power-outages

Atlantic City Electric outages by town, with customers out, percent out and estimated restoration. A pin marks the town center, not the outage itself.

Map key
Blue 0, yellow 1–49, orange 50–499, red 500+ customers out.
Source
ACE public outage map, every 5 min.

Readiness

readiness

Ready.gov / FEMA checklists for a go-bag and home kit. Items turn "due now" when a live layer is active, such as an NWS warning, flooding tide, outages, drought or a geomagnetic storm.

Note
Your checkmarks stay on your device only.

County risk

county-risk

A 0–100 score computed by fixed rules from NWS alerts, tides, outages, GDACS, earthquakes, fires, lightning and space weather. It shows each input and lists any that are missing.

Bands
Low 0–19, Guarded 20–39, Elevated 40–59 (yellow), High 60–79 (orange), Severe 80+ (red).
Note
A missing input is never counted as low risk.

Hazards & Infrastructure 12 LAYERS

Earthquakes (24h)

earthquakes

Magnitude 2.5+ earthquakes worldwide in the last day. Tap for magnitude, depth, time and the USGS page.

Source
USGS, every minute.

FIRMS Active Fires

local-firms

Satellite heat detections. Most are controlled burns or industry; confirm before calling it a wildfire.

Fire Perimeters

fire-perimeters

Mapped wildfire perimeters with InciWeb incident pages.

Source
NIFC WFIGS, every 5 min.

GDACS Hazards

gdacs-hazards

Worldwide disasters (quakes, cyclones, floods, volcanoes, wildfires, droughts) with GDACS's own green, orange or red alert level.

Source
GDACS (UN / EU), every 15 min.

Outbreaks

outbreaks

A county label with New Jersey West Nile counts and Cape May County activity, plus WHO disease-outbreak headlines in the panel.

Source
CDC ArboNET and WHO, daily.

Radiation

radiation

Background gamma dose rate at the nearest EPA monitors (Philadelphia and Edison), with Safecast readings. Regional background, never a county reading.

Source
EPA RadNet and Safecast.

Fallout Plume

fallout-plume

Which way a release from Salem / Hope Creek would drift. The NWS hourly forecast wind steps a 15° cone downwind for 24 hours, around a 10-mile planning-zone circle.

Limits
Wind direction only. Not a dispersion model (not HYSPLIT) and not a dose estimate. Protective actions come from NJ OEM.

Internet Outages

internet-outages

Internet connectivity drops for New Jersey and the U.S., plus the worst global events.

Source
IODA (Georgia Tech), every 5 min.

Disaster history

disaster-history

34 federal disaster declarations for the county since 1965 (latest: Ida remnants, 2021), plus NOAA storm events, as a timeline.

Source
OpenFEMA and NOAA NCEI.

HIFLD Installations

hifld-installations

744 U.S. military installations. Green active, grey closed, amber other.

Source
A frozen 2023 snapshot; HIFLD Open shut down in 2025.

Dams

local-dams

Dams from the national inventory, for inland flooding questions.

Source
USACE National Inventory of Dams.

Submarine Cables

telegeography-submarine-cables

Undersea internet cables and landing points. Several land on the NJ shore.

Source
TeleGeography.

Space, Views, Briefings & Utilities 10 LAYERS

Satellites

satellites

Live satellite positions from eleven CelesTrak groups, including the space stations, GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, geostationary, weather and Earth-imaging satellites. Starlink has its own button because it is so dense.

Source
CelesTrak orbits, computed in your browser.

Space Missions (30d)

rocket-launches

Launches in the last and next 30 days. Wallops launches can be visible from Cape May.

Source
Launch Library 2.

SatNOGS Observations

satnogs

What the 4 amateur satellite ground stations within 200 km heard in the last week, and what they will listen for in the next 48 h.

Street Level

street-level

Street photos where you click: Mapillary, KartaView, Panoramax or Google Street View. Coverage lines show where photos exist.

Recent Imagery

recent-imagery

Draw a box and list the last 30 days of Sentinel-2 / Landsat and VIIRS satellite pictures over it; pin a day to drape it on the map. Good for before-and-after storm damage.

Source
NASA GIBS.

Daily brief

daily-brief

A written summary of the last 24 hours of county news and alerts, with a confidence level. A stale brief always shows its date.

History replay

history-replay

Scrub back in time: stored aircraft positions and feed items at any moment.

Credibility scores

credibility-scoring

News stories grouped into clusters and scored by fixed rules in the news service. The top five are labeled with score, band, trend and the main reasons for the score.

Directions

directions

Turn-by-turn driving route between two points, each step tappable on the map.

Source
OpenStreetMap routing.

OSM Places

osm-places

Results of a voice search such as "hospitals near Wildwood", drawn as labeled points.

Situations PLAYBOOKS

Turn layers on in the order listed. Each playbook says what to look for and where to confirm. The site informs; County OEM, the NWS and the FAA decide.

Coastal storm and tidal flooding

nor'easter, full-moon high tides

Turn on

  1. NWS Alerts: Coastal Flood Advisory, Watch or Warning
  2. Tide & Flood Gauges: Cape May Harbor, Ocean City, Stone Harbor, back bays
  3. Predicted Flooding: where water would reach at the forecast peak
  4. Marine Buoys: 44009 wind and waves
  5. Wind stations and Wind
  6. Traffic Incidents for flooded roads
  7. Webcams to see it

Look for

  • A gauge turning yellow (action), then orange (minor), red (moderate) or purple (major) at its own flood stages
  • A forecast peak category above the observed one: it's getting worse
  • Persistent NE wind at 44009 piling water into the back bays across several tide cycles
  • Orange Predicted Flooding over evacuation routes: Route 47, Route 9, the Ocean Drive bridges, causeways to the barrier islands

Confirm with

  • NWS Mount Holly
  • NWS Cape May Harbor gauge
  • Cape May County OEM

Can't see

Street-level flooding until it's reported. Radar Flood only after a satellite pass.

Hurricane or tropical storm approach

days out to landfall

Turn on

  1. Cyclone advisories: track and cone
  2. NWS Alerts: tropical and storm-surge watches and warnings
  3. Tide & Flood Gauges, Predicted Flooding
  4. Satellite clouds, Rain radar
  5. Readiness: what's due now
  6. County risk
  7. Power Outages after arrival
  8. Fuel Prices and Transit before evacuation

Look for

  • A track passing west of the county puts it on the storm's stronger right side
  • Surge forecasts at the Cape May and Atlantic City gauges
  • Readiness items flipping to "due now"
  • Outage counts climbing past 500 in a town (red)

Confirm with

  • National Hurricane Center
  • Cape May County OEM evacuation orders
  • NJ OEM

Can't see

Shelters and official evacuation zones.

Severe thunderstorm, lightning, tornado warning

summer afternoons and fronts

Turn on

  1. NWS Alerts: red polygons are warnings
  2. Rain radar
  3. Lightning density
  4. Airport Weather: IFR and gusts at KWWD, KACY, KMIV
  5. Webcams

Look for

  • A warning polygon over a town (red, magenta for extreme)
  • Lightning density moving toward beaches and boardwalks; ACY or KWWD gust groups in the METAR
  • County risk counting lightning flashes in the last 30–60 min

Confirm with

  • NWS Mount Holly
  • NOAA Weather Radio

Can't see

Damage reports until residents send them or NWS publishes storm reports.

Aircraft emergency or accident

near KWWD, KACY, or offshore

Turn on

  1. Live Flights: look for a 7700, 7600 or 7500 squawk flag, then tap it
  2. Airport Radio (ATC): the tower transcript and clip at ACY
  3. Airport Weather: conditions at the field
  4. Military Flights: USCG helicopters launching from Air Station Atlantic City
  5. Local TFRs: a 91.137 TFR usually follows an accident
  6. FAA Airport Status: closure or ground stop at ACY or PHL
  7. Live AIS Vessels for an offshore ditching

Look for

  • Squawk 7700 emergency, 7600 radio failure, 7500 unlawful interference
  • Tower transcript keywords flagged red; listen, because transcripts mishear
  • The aircraft disappearing near the coast may only be coverage; check VDL2 and Local ADS-B
  • A new TFR over the site within the hour

Confirm with

  • FAA NAS Status
  • FAA TFR list
  • NOTAM search

Can't see

Non-transponder aircraft, low GA traffic out of feed coverage, and the dispatch side.

Airspace disruption or GPS interference

VIP movement, jamming, closures

Turn on

  1. Local TFRs
  2. Prohibited & Restricted Airspace
  3. GPSJam (Daily)
  4. Space weather
  5. FAA Airport Status
  6. SIGMETs for the offshore FIR

Look for

  • 91.141 VIP TFRs: rings of 10 and 30 NM around a protected person
  • Yellow or red GPSJam cells over the shore; ADS-B aircraft reporting low NIC and NACp
  • A geomagnetic storm (Kp 5 and up, worse at 7+) can degrade GPS accuracy and HF radio
  • A ground stop or GDP at PHL or EWR pushing traffic over the county

Confirm with

  • NOTAM search for GPS interference NOTAMs
  • USCG NAVCEN GPS status
  • NOAA SWPC

Can't see

Warning areas, MOAs and their activation times. Live jamming: GPSJam is yesterday's picture.

Vessel in distress or search and rescue

offshore, Delaware Bay, the ferry

Turn on

  1. Live AIS Vessels: find the vessel by name, last course and speed
  2. Marine Buoys: waves, wind, water temperature
  3. Marine Warnings: Coast Guard broadcast notices
  4. NWS Alerts: small craft and gale warnings for the coastal waters and the bay
  5. Military Flights: USCG helicopter search patterns
  6. Right Whale Speed Zones

Look for

  • An AIS track that stops or goes silent while others nearby keep reporting
  • Water temperature for survival time: the 50s °F mean cold-water shock
  • Wave height and period at 44009 for sea state
  • A helicopter flying a search pattern offshore

Confirm with

  • USCG NAVCEN notices
  • Cape May–Lewes Ferry for ferry status
  • Emergencies: VHF channel 16 or 911

Can't see

Boats without AIS, which is most recreational boats.

Major crash, gridlock or evacuation traffic

Parkway, Route 47, Route 55, Route 9

Turn on

  1. Traffic Incidents: crashes and closures
  2. Street Traffic: speeds
  3. Traffic cameras and Webcams
  4. Directions to try an alternate route
  5. Fuel Prices before a long drive

Look for

  • Red incidents (crash or road closed) on the Parkway, Route 47/347 or the Route 9 bridges
  • Delay minutes and expected end time on the incident card
  • Red flow stretching back from a closure

Confirm with

  • 511NJ
  • NJ Turnpike Authority for the Parkway

Can't see

Crashes nobody has reported to TomTom yet, and the dispatch side of the crash.

Power outage

storms, heat, equipment

Turn on

  1. Power Outages: town totals
  2. The ticker's POWER item for the county total
  3. NWS Alerts for the cause
  4. Readiness

Look for

  • Towns turning orange (50+) or red (500+ out)
  • Estimated restoration time on the town card
  • Outages spreading in the same direction as a storm on radar

Confirm with

  • ACE outage map

Can't see

Individual streets. Pins are town centers.

Wildfire or Pine Barrens smoke

spring fire season, dry spells

Turn on

  1. FIRMS Active Fires
  2. Fire Perimeters
  3. Wind
  4. Air quality
  5. Drought
  6. NWS Alerts: red flag warnings, air quality alerts

Look for

  • Clustered FIRMS detections in the Pine Barrens or Belleplain State Forest
  • Wind carrying smoke toward the county
  • AQI orange or worse
  • Drought D1 or worse raises fire danger

Confirm with

  • NJ Forest Fire Service
  • AirNow
  • InciWeb

Can't see

Small fires under the satellite's detection size, and controlled burns look like wildfires.

Police or fire incident

something is happening on your block

Turn on

  1. Police, Fire & EMS Calls and CAD incidents, when the scanner feed is running
  2. Community Reports
  3. Emergency Facilities: nearest hospital, fire and EMS stations
  4. Hydrants (ops desk) for the nearest hydrant and its color
  5. Traffic Incidents for road closures

Look for

  • Red high-priority calls and units heard
  • Several reports from the same area

Confirm with

  • The responding agency's official posts
  • Cape May County OEM

Can't see

Anything the scanner didn't hear. Calls are machine-transcribed and unconfirmed.

Nuclear plant or hazmat release

Salem / Hope Creek, about 40–50 miles northwest

Turn on

  1. NWS Alerts: civil emergency messages
  2. Fallout Plume: which way the wind carries it
  3. Wind
  4. Radiation: EPA monitors
  5. Readiness

Look for

  • A plume cone pointing southeast toward the county
  • Radiation monitors well above their normal readings
  • The county is far outside the 10-mile plume planning zone and near the edge of the 50-mile ingestion zone, where food and water advice applies

Confirm with

  • NJ OEM and Emergency Alert System broadcasts

Can't see

Dose. The plume is wind direction only, not a dispersion model.

Earthquake or tsunami

rare here, but check fast

Turn on

  1. Earthquakes (24h)
  2. NWS Alerts: tsunami messages
  3. Tide & Flood Gauges: sudden jumps
  4. GDACS Hazards

Look for

  • A large Atlantic or Caribbean earthquake
  • Gauges jumping out of step with the tide

Confirm with

  • tsunami.gov
  • USGS

Can't see

Wave arrival forecasts. Use tsunami.gov.

Heat wave or poor air

summer crowds, ozone, smoke

Turn on

  1. NWS Alerts: heat advisories and warnings
  2. Air quality
  3. Airport Weather temperatures
  4. Power Outages: grid strain
  5. Emergency Facilities: hospitals

Look for

  • Yellow heat advisory going red (warning)
  • AQI orange or worse in the afternoon
  • Scattered outages on the hottest evenings

Confirm with

  • NWS Mount Holly
  • AirNow

Can't see

Cooling-center locations. Get those from the county.

World events and preparedness

conflict, outages, outbreaks, the wider picture

Turn on

  1. Daily brief
  2. Credibility scores: which stories have independent, official sourcing
  3. GDACS Hazards
  4. Internet Outages
  5. Outbreaks
  6. Submarine Cables
  7. County risk and Readiness
  8. Space weather, Radiation
  9. Fuel Prices

Look for

  • Red GDACS events and IODA outages hitting the region or a cable landing
  • Stories with high credibility and a rising trend, not just loud ones
  • Fuel prices jumping week over week
  • Readiness telling you what to top up

Confirm with

  • Ready.gov
  • GDACS

Morning check 2 MINUTES

  1. Read the ticker. Every item colored and none muted means all five county feeds are fresh. A grey dash is a feed problem, not good news.
  2. Open Local → Alerts. Any NWS product for the county or the coastal waters?
  3. Tide & Flood Gauges. Any yellow or worse, and is the forecast peak category higher than now?
  4. Airport Weather and FAA Airport Status. KWWD and KACY flight categories; any ground stop or GDP at PHL or EWR.
  5. Local TFRs. Anything new over the county.
  6. Power Outages. Any orange or red towns.
  7. Daily brief and Credibility scores. What happened overnight, and which stories hold up.
